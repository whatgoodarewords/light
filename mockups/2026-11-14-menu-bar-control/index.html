A control for the lamp in the menu bar

These are mock-ups, not a built app. The pick is a small panel that opens under a lamp icon in the menu bar. It would use the same helper as the light command, so both always agree on what the lamp is doing.

Three ways it could look are below. Say which one, and what to change, before anything gets built.

The pick: a panel under a menu bar icon

One click from anywhere. Sliders for brightness and warmth, your looks as swatches, the moving modes as buttons.

What building the pick involves

  • A small native macOS app in Swift, living next to the light files. It sends the same requests the command sends, so nothing about the lamp protocol is duplicated.
  • No new Bluetooth permission: the existing helper already has it.
  • Optional: start at login. Off unless you turn it on.
  • While the panel is open the helper stays connected, so the Govee app cannot reach the bulb during that time. It lets go 45 seconds after you close the panel.
