The `light` command at /opt/homebrew/bin/light now runs version 2 from this
folder. On, off, brightness, colour, Kelvin, named looks, saved looks, candle,

Verification evidence (files are in work/evidence-v2/)

1. Identity. 02-scan.jsonl: one device named GVH6008DBA0, a second H6008
   (another bulb of the same model, weak signal) also in range and never touched. 03-watch.jsonl:
   after the request to unplug, GVH6008DBA0 went silent at 18:07:54 for 63.4
   seconds while 49 other devices were still heard, then returned under the same
   identifier, 6D60E3AC-795E-3D7D-980A-FBBB1E7652B3, which is also the one
   version 1 pinned. The owner's reply to the unplug request was "Great. And
   yeah"; there is no explicit "I unplugged it" on record.

2. Protocol. This bulb uses the two-step E7 handshake with AES-ECB/RC4, as
   version 1 found. It has no ...2b12 version characteristic and does not use
   the AES-GCM transport of homebridge-govee or govee-homeassistant.

3. Every lasting change is checked twice by the tool itself: the bulb's
   acknowledgement, then a separate state readback that must match.

4. Independent of the bulb's own word: the MacBook's ambient light sensor
   (tests/lux.py, no camera). In the dark room it read 0 to 2 with the lamp off
   and 192 at white 2700K 100%, and followed dimming, the candle, breathe,
   sunset and sunrise.

5. Offline tests: 60 pass (.venv/bin/python -m unittest discover -s tests).
   Two of them were seen failing before their fix: the final brightness after a
   glide was not re-verified, and `off` trusted a stale state.

6. Live runs with the installed command (tests/live_check.py --long):
   live-check-installed.log            39 of 40. The miss was the check reading
                                       a sunrise that started 17 s late.
   live-check-run2-before-direct-connect.log   40 of 40, dark room.
   live-check-final.log                38 of 40, on the final files
                                       (candidate-sha256.txt). All bulb
                                       readbacks passed. The two misses are
                                       sensor steps for breathe and sunset: from
                                       about 19:20 another light was on in the
                                       room (sensor near 180 with this lamp at
                                       70% orange, which alone reads 14). Those
                                       two steps passed by sensor in run 2; the
                                       code they exercise did not change between
                                       run 2 and the final run. The check now
                                       skips such steps and says so.

7. Reliability fix found by testing: listening for Bluetooth advertisements
   missed the lamp for up to 30 seconds at a time in this building, and one
   `light status` failed that way. The helper now connects by the lamp's known
   identifier first (12-direct-connect.log: 2.6 s, then 1.3 s straight after a
   disconnect) and listens only as a fallback. After the change, three cold
   starts took 2.2, 1.7 and 1.7 seconds, and the log shows no fallback used
   during the final run.

8. Measured limits (06-scale-probe.log, 09-kelvin-probe.log): white below 2700K
   is clamped to 2700K (the bulb reports 2700K and the sensor reads the same
   light); the colour mixes give about a fifth of the light of real white; any
   setting wakes the bulb; darker colour values dim it smoothly.

Material caveats

- Nobody has judged the candle, breathe or the fades by eye. The sensor shows
  they move as designed; whether they look good is the owner's call. Tuning
  knobs: light candle calm | lively, and the Flame class in warm.py.
- Bulb scenes: the bulb reports the selected scene and the sensor shows fire
  and candlelight moving, but whether the uploaded animation data or a stored
  default is playing was not separated.
- Not tested: Mac asleep or lid closed during a timer or sunrise; the lamp
  losing power mid-mode; a first run on a Mac that has never met the lamp
  (the listening fallback); Bluetooth permission denied or Bluetooth off
  (messages written, not exercised).
- The helper uses internals of bleak 3.1.0 to connect by identifier.
  requirements.txt pins that version.
- Light.app is signed with the local Apple Development certificate, not
  notarized. It and the lamp identifier are specific to this Mac.
- No independent model audit was run. The tool handles no personal data.
